About Mbele ECD

Welcome to Mbele ECD English Creche

Mbele ECD English Creche is an early childhood development and education centre at 12518 Nomane Street, Tokoza. It is a welcoming place for young children to learn, play and grow.

We believe children learn best when curiosity, creativity and play come together. There is time here for discovery, imagination and the confidence that grows when children take part, make friends and express themselves.

Learning is joyful and unhurried. Through meaningful activities and play, children explore the world around them and find their place in a caring group.
